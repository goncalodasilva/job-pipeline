Delete all data?

This deletes everything inside the app's folders: your data, documents, imports, assets, prompts, backups, exports and logs. It can't be undone.

Export a copy first if you want to keep your work. Exports and backups are inside the app's folders and are deleted too, so download the export and keep it somewhere else.

  • Keys the app saved in your computer's keychain are removed too. They are shared by every workspace on this computer, unless the app's setup file points at another data folder.
  • A key you set as an environment variable can't be removed by the app. Remove it from your environment yourself.
  • Deleted files are not securely overwritten, so backups on your computer, such as Time Machine, may keep copies.
