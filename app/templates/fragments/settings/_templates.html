Needs `cv_templates` (app/api/settings_templates.py). It holds no personal data, so the token-free Settings page carries it. The list refreshes itself on the body's `templates-changed` event (a template was saved, approved or drafted in the editor or on an application's CV step) with a GET that changes nothing and takes no focus; only the list is replaced, never the editor. Review loads the country's unit into the editor in place and moves focus to its heading. #}

CV templates

A CV template says which sections a CV has and in what order, how long it is, whether it has a photo slot, which contact details it shows and how dates look. You draft one per country from an application's Tailoring tab, review every field, then approve it. Nothing about you is sent to the AI.
