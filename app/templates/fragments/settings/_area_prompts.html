Prompts

Each AI task follows a prompt: the instructions the app sends with your text. You can change the wording of any of them. Your version is kept on this computer and used instead of the default until you reset it.

A poorly worded prompt can make results worse or less reliable. If that happens, use Reset to go back to the default.

The app always adds its own notice that text from outside the app is untrusted, and its honesty rule for anything it writes about you. You can't remove them.
