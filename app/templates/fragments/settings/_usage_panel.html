One block per service: the count that is used, the status in words (the bar repeats it, never alone), the reset date, the blocking point and tier caps, unfinished calls, both counts when the provider reports one that can be compared, and requests and tokens by task type and tier in a table that scrolls inside its own container. It only shows: the budget forms follow it. It fetches itself again after a budget was saved or removed and, debounced, when a task changes (never on tasks:resync, which every stream connection fires). A refresh is silent: nothing in it takes focus or is announced, and its ids are stable, so htmx keeps focus on a breakdown region across the swap. #}
