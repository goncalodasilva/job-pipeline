Usage budgets

Optional. A budget is a number of requests per month for one service. You get a warning at each of two counts, and at the blocking point the app stops using that service until the reset day. A tier cap limits Fast or Thorough tasks on their own. A service without a budget has no limit.

The second warning must be higher than the first, and the blocking point higher than the second. The blocking point can equal the allowance.
