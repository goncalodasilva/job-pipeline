{# Why an action can't start (M2.24; AC-M2.7): the service is at its blocking point, a tier is at its cap with nothing to offer, or the stored budgets can't be read. Generic: `budget_blocked` comes from app/api/budget_gate.py (reason_context). It goes above the action's button, which names `budget_blocked.id` in its aria-describedby. At the blocking point the button also carries aria-disabled="true": it stays reachable by keyboard, so the reason is read; app.js keeps it from doing anything and says the reason when it is pressed, and the server refuses the start anyway. The link goes to the budgets; on the page that holds them, app.js moves focus to that section's heading (data-section-link). Rule: a page that shows a blocked action and can change budgets must refresh it on `usage:changed` (the body event a saved or removed budget fires), with a GET that changes nothing and moves no focus, and never while the refreshed part holds an open dialog. Settings > Testing tools does (fragments/settings/_testing_tools.html). #}
