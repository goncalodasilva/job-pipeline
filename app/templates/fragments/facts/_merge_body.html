{# The merge screen's body (M3.6): what every form on the screen replaces. Needs `merge` (a MergeView, or none with a `conflict`), `draft_id`, `said`, `conflict`, `reload_url`, `back_url` (a refusal that a reload would only repeat, such as a fact that isn't a draft any more: the link leads to the Drafts tab instead) and `searched`. search: the draft, a GET search form (with htmx it replaces the screen's content, the address follows, and focus moves to the candidates' heading: `searched`) and the facts it can be merged into, each with a "Compare" link. The search swaps itself, its field included, so it settles at once (settle:0ms): htmx otherwise gives the new field the old one's attributes for a moment, and what is typed in that moment is overwritten. compare: both wordings (side by side on a wide screen, stacked on a phone) and the form that settles the wording; the fields for a wording of one's own are always there, so the screen needs no script. result: where the draft went and, while the merge can be taken back, "Undo merge". One focus target: the result, the line an undo left, the reload prompt or a refused field. Everything is escaped. #}
