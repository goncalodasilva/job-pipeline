## Manual checklist — left for the user

Use a fresh temporary workspace outside the repository:

```bash
uv run jobpipeline --data-dir "$(mktemp -d)"
```

Connect your own provider yourself. Do not commit postings, screenshots, notes, logs or
exports, and keep credentials out of the repository. The agent has not performed any of these
checks.

- [ ] Add confirmed facts first (type a few, or import a fictional CV), so fit can run. Then triage a few real postings: add some by link and some by pasting the ad text, and press through the queue. Check that each card shows what the posting states (title, company, industry, department or team, work mode, salary, experience, posting age) and nothing it does not. Check the summaries, the requirement lists and the fit bands against the postings with your own judgment (TD-240, TD-241).
- [ ] Add a job by link with a real provider connected, and again by pasting the text. Note which job boards refuse the fetch or answer with a page without job text, and whether the paste fallback feels clear (TD-225). Check that Adding a job warns "up to 2 requests" when a budget warning applies and that a blocked request leaves the saved job triageable (TD-055).
- [ ] Look at the inbox, the job drawer, `/jobs/{id}`, the values-to-map page and the companies list and company page at desktop width and at phone width (360 and 390 px), in light and dark themes.
- [ ] Triage by keyboard only: J, K, A, R (with a reason chip and with Other), Enter, Z, Esc, the card menu snooze and `?`. Listen to the announcements with a screen reader if you use one. In Settings > Keyboard shortcuts, turn "Use single-key shortcuts" off and check that nothing happens on single keys, that every action still has a button and that `?` still opens the help; turn it on again.
- [ ] Check the filters, group-by, search, saved views (create, rename, delete, open) and the Snoozed, Removed and To apply views. Check that a snoozed card returns as new on its date (set it for tomorrow and check the next day, or accept the automated clock test).
- [ ] Correct a field in the drawer (salary or industry), then press "Summarize again" and check your correction is kept and marked "Corrected by you". Open a fact from "How you match" and check the wording shown is your own current wording. Change that fact and check the drawer notes the change.
- [ ] Map an unknown value on the values page (map it to an existing value, add a new one, ignore another) and check the jobs update as described.
- [ ] Try a duplicate: add the same ad from two boards (or paste it twice). Check it merges as another source, then use "Not a duplicate" and check it stays split.
- [ ] Rename a company and add a job for the old name: confirm it creates a new company, as the README says (TD-257). Check the company page's website link, notes and job list.
- [ ] Confirm TD-254: open `/inbox` with your own jobs in the temporary folder. The earlier unverified "Internal Server Error" on `/inbox` was never reproduced. Either reproduce it (note the steps and the traceback from the data folder's `logs/`) or record that you could not and close TD-254.
- [ ] Live or real-provider checks (nothing was run live in M4): confirm the real service accepts the `summarize_job` and `assess_fit` schemas and returns useful output (TD-263), and that a long posting does not time out. The spec lists no other live test for M4. The M1 live checks (TD-120 to TD-123) are still unrun.
- [ ] Run a dependency advisory lookup for the locked `httpx`, `httpcore` and `h11` (and the earlier `pypdf`, `lxml` and `python-multipart`) (TD-001).
- [ ] After you authorize a push, get a green CI run for the final M4.11 commit and record its URL in the Verification section above. No push or merge was performed by this workflow.

## Limits and follow-ups

The complete open register is [TECH_DEBT.md](../TECH_DEBT.md). This report does not close
anything by implication. Key limits for acceptance:

- Fetching reads one public page at a time. The default User-Agent may be refused by some boards (TD-225); an https page may redirect to http (TD-226); any port on a public address is accepted (TD-227); only the first DNS answer is tried (TD-231); the demo can reach the network for hosts other than `*.example.test` (TD-233, needs your confirmation).
- Fit is a guide. The app checks that the facts a match cites were really sent, not that the match is true or that a preference conflict is a real contradiction (TD-241). The band is computed by the app, never by the AI.
- Summary checks are conservative: salary, experience and posted date are kept only when the posting states them in a form the app recognises, so valid but unusual wording leaves a field empty (TD-240). Correct it in the drawer.
- Company merge and requirement editing are not built (TD-257, TD-258). Renaming a company changes how a later job for the old name is matched. The company list loads whole per request (TD-259), and the page title does not follow a rename until reload (TD-260).
- Group-by counts the whole filter but lists the current page (TD-261). Adding a job refreshes the inbox without the typed-text guard (TD-242). Remove needs JavaScript (TD-252).
- Retry from the task panel skips the budget warning (TD-171), and the budget gate checks only the next request (TD-055, narrowed in M4).
- Snapshots, summaries, notes and removal reasons stay in the local database and its backups; there is no erase path yet (TD-264; TD-010 and TD-011 for facts).
- Out of M4 by decision (ADR-098 item 7): learning from removals and auto-hide (M9), the command palette (M9), company contacts and history (M5), the company brief (M8) and the watch toggle (M10). Apply marks a job "To apply"; M5 creates the application.

Decisions waiting for you, from the M4 notes: whether the demo may fetch from the real
network (TD-233); whether to add the fetcher to the critical-coverage registry (TD-230);
whether Retry should go through the budget gate (TD-171); and the audit step for
dependencies (TD-001).
