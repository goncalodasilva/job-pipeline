## Manual checklist — left for the user

Use a fresh temporary workspace, outside the repository, for your own CV:

```bash
uv run jobpipeline --data-dir "$(mktemp -d)"
```

Choose and connect your provider yourself. Keep the CV, screenshots, exports, credentials
and personal logs out of this repository. The agent has not performed these checks.

- [ ] With a fictional CV first, check the real provider accepts the extraction schema and returns useful drafts. Check one refine conversation, including questions, your answer and a suggestion. Confirm structured-output support, output limits and the provider assumptions in TD-175; use your own credentials without sharing them here.
- [ ] Import your own PDF or Word CV (or paste its text). Review quotes, roles, stories, skills and needs-check markers. Confirm that nothing was confirmed automatically; check the quality of multi-column/table extraction. Word headers, footers and text boxes are not extracted (TD-028).
- [ ] Confirm/edit a draft, merge another, discard and undo, and batch-confirm a source. Check history and search/filter results. Review any duplicate role suggestions; roles are not deduplicated automatically.
- [ ] Refine a fact. Review the whole diff and the context-count line. Confirm the claim checkboxes and explicit Accept, and listen for a single completion announcement. Try Never use and Generalize names. Generalization sends source names to the provider and controls wording only.
- [ ] Check the Fact bank and refine panel by keyboard and at phone width in light/dark themes. Check the budget warning dialog with Continue/Not now and the hard-block reason (TD-199).
- [ ] Skip Import CV in the setup guide and check its link on Done; return and import. Skipped steps will appear on Today in M5.
- [ ] Run a dependency advisory audit covering the locked `pypdf`, `python-docx`/`lxml` and multipart parser (TD-001). No dependency audit is included in verification.
- [ ] After you authorize a push, obtain green CI for the M3.10 commit and record its URL here. No push or merge was performed by this workflow.
- [ ] Confirm acceptance, or identify fixes needed before accepting M3.

## Limits and follow-ups

The complete open register is [TECH_DEBT.md](../TECH_DEBT.md), including items from all
M3 tasks and inherited M1/M2 live checks. This report does not close them by implication.
Key limits for acceptance:

- Discard/archive/remove are not erasure: versions, events, capture notes, closed refine conversations, source snapshots and backups retain text (TD-010/011). Removing an upload keeps extracted drafts and roles.
- The honesty checker cannot catch every unsupported phrase. Lower-case non-numeric embellishment and a lone capitalized word at a sentence start can escape it. Number-word checks are deliberately strict (ADR-097); the user accepted that tradeoff. Confirmed acknowledgements stay in the accepted fact, without a separate draft.
- The demo's fake suggestions are fixed fictional scripts, even for unrelated facts (TD-223). Review the wording; a Practice data badge follows the current wording and each history version.
- Saving the editor clears a typed refine answer (TD-221). A failed turn retaining an earlier suggestion does not refresh after Retry from Tasks until reload (TD-222). Task retry enforces the hard budget limit but bypasses the start-route warning dialog (ADR-097).
- Search uses whole words without stemming, prefix matching or skill-alias indexing. Some role cleanup/deduplication, parser memory limits and slow-upload protections remain deferred. Parser children have deadlines and CPU limits; a hard app kill can leave one alive until its limit.
- Generation/tailoring, Coverage, used-in-application links, tag filtering, quality hints, fact shortcuts and per-task tier settings are later milestone work (ADR-089).

No new dependency, schema, stored personal-data field, honesty rule or security model was
introduced by M3.10. Acceptance and advancing Current milestone still require the user's
confirmation.
